4 West Escape Room

You are the oncoming shift on 4 West. The day nurse left in a hurry and locked the medication handoff report in a box. She left eight clues around the unit. Each station gives you one letter of the code word. Collect all eight, then take the word to your instructor to open the lockbox.

  1. Finish the task at a station to unlock its clue question.
  2. The first letter of each clue answer goes into the lock.
  3. Stations can be done in any order. Checking an answer tells you how many you have right, not which ones.
